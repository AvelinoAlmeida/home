My bike

KTM Macina
Chacana 791

The KTM Macina Chacana 791 26" is much more than a bike: it is a statement about responsible mobility. With the Bosch Performance CX motor, FOX suspension and a 750 Wh battery, I explore the Vale do Sousa trails with a range and comfort that a conventional bike could never offer.

Every trail ridden instead of a car journey means less CO₂, less noise and better-preserved natural spaces around Penafiel.
